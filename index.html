Capital Media HQ

Capital Media HQ is the small private tool behind three sports pages: Fight Capital Media (MMA and boxing), Ice Capital Media (hockey) and Blitz Capital Media (football).

It is used only by the owner of these pages, Matthew Sartor, to schedule and publish his own videos and posts to his own YouTube, Instagram, Facebook and TikTok accounts, and to read the basic numbers (views, likes, followers) for those same accounts.

It is not a public app. Nobody else can sign in to it.
