Privacy policy

Capital Media HQ · last updated November 23, 2026

Who this is for

Capital Media HQ is a private tool used only by Matthew Sartor to manage his own social media accounts (Fight Capital Media, Ice Capital Media, Blitz Capital Media). It has no other users.

What data it uses

When the owner connects one of his accounts (Google/YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok), the tool receives an access token from that platform. With it, the tool:

  • uploads and publishes videos and posts the owner prepared,
  • reads basic numbers for those accounts (views, likes, comments, followers) to make a weekly report,
  • on Google Drive, creates a temporary file only to hand a photo to Instagram, then deletes it.

It does not read other people's private data, messages, contacts or email.

Where it is kept

Access tokens and reports are stored only on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Google user data

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube data is used under the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time: Google at myaccount.google.com/permissions, Instagram/Facebook in Settings > Apps and websites, TikTok in Settings > Security > Manage app permissions. Deleting the tokens on the owner's computer also removes all stored data.
